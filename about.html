We work where the tender ends and the operating years begin.

A civil and environmental infrastructure service company built around the realities of public-sector delivery in Tamil Nadu.

01, Who we are

End-to-end support for municipal infrastructure.

RD Eco Solutions is based in Sirkazhi, Mayiladuthurai District. We provide end-to-end support for municipal infrastructure projects, from planning and tender consultancy through execution, operation, maintenance and performance monitoring.

We focus on scopes that can actually be delivered on the site and the budget in front of us, and operated afterwards on the recurring budget the body actually has.

02, The standard

Finding operators is not the difficulty. Keeping the deployment documented is where these contracts go wrong, and where the department ends up exposed.

Attendance · Wage records · EPF & ESI · Disposal documentation
03, Why us

What departments get.

  • Public-sector delivery. Municipal infrastructure work under the documentation and audit conditions that come with it.
  • End-to-end support. From DPR and tender documents through execution to long-run operation and maintenance.
  • Experienced technical team. Engineers, supervisors and operators who have run these systems before.
  • Zero manual entry. Mechanised cleaning and confined-space protocol on every sewerage deployment, without exception.
  • Documented at every stage. Daily logs, measurement records, disposal documentation, the paper trail an audit asks for.
  • Practical scoping. Scoped to the site, the budget, and what the body can sustain after handover.
  • Environmentally responsible. Operations designed around resource recovery, reuse and TNPCB compliance.
