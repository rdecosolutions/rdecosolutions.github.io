Project Consultancy

Municipal projects are approved, funded and awarded on the strength of their paperwork. We prepare the reports, estimates and tender documents that carry a scheme through sanction and procurement — built on site conditions and current schedules of rates rather than on templates.

Enquire about this service All services
Why it matters

Why the documentation stage decides the project

A DPR that under-states quantities becomes a cost overrun two years into execution, and a bid document with gaps is rejected at technical evaluation before anyone reads the price. Getting the estimate, the specification and the annexures right at this stage is the cheapest work on the whole project — and the most expensive to get wrong.

Typical engagements
  • Detailed Project Report for a new scheme going for sanction
  • Feasibility study before a budget is committed
  • Tender and bid-document preparation for a work about to be floated
  • Independent review of an existing estimate or BOQ
  • Technical proposal support for a contractor bidding a municipal work
